# Image attribution

Every downloaded photograph in `public/images/` comes from Wikimedia Commons and
is used under a public-domain dedication or a Creative Commons licence. The
tables below record the source, author and licence for each, as those licences
require.

Images that shipped with the project before this file existed (`fruits/`,
`foods/` and two `finger-count` images) are unchanged and not listed.

Regenerate with `tools/fetch-images.py`, `tools/fetch-new.py` and
`tools/wire-images.py`; `tools/image-manifest.json` is the item-to-file map the
app data is generated from.
